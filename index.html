Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who is the Data Controller?

This waiting list is managed by an individual developer based in Italy (EU). For any privacy-related requests, contact: screeningclearing@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

  • Your email address
  • Date and time of your submission (timestamp)
  • Your IP address
  • Your browser and device information (user agent)
  • The exact consent text you agreed to (version 1.0)

3. Why we collect it (Legal basis)

We collect your data solely to add you to the Screening Clearing waiting list and to notify you when the product launches. The legal basis is your freely given, specific and informed consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

4. How long we keep it

We retain your data until you withdraw your consent or request deletion, or until 24 months from the date of collection — whichever comes first.

5. Who we share it with

Your data is stored in Google Sheets (Google LLC, operating under EU Standard Contractual Clauses). It is not sold, rented or shared with any third party for marketing purposes.

6. Your rights (GDPR Art. 15–22)

  • Access — you can ask what data we hold about you
  • Rectification — you can ask us to correct your data
  • Erasure — you can ask us to delete your data at any time
  • Withdrawal of consent — you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Lodge a complaint — you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Garante (www.garanteprivacy.it) or your local supervisory authority

7. How to exercise your rights

Send an email to screeningclearing@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This page does not use cookies or tracking technologies.
